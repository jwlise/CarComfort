CarComfort Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-18

CarComfort respects and values your privacy. This app is primarily used to measure in-vehicle vibration, ride comfort, and motion sickness risk trends. The following explains how we handle data.

1. What Data We Collect

During measurements, CarComfort uses the device’s motion sensor data, including:

  • Acceleration and device motion data
  • Measurement start and end times
  • Vehicle and route names entered by the user in the app

2. How Data Is Used

The data above is used only to:

  • Calculate vehicle comfort, motion sickness risk, and vibration metrics
  • Display measurement results in the app
  • Generate and export PDF reports

3. What We Do Not Do

CarComfort does not:

  • Create user accounts
  • Automatically upload measurement data to the developer’s servers
  • Track users’ activity across apps or websites
  • Collect contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera data

4. Data Storage

Measurement data and generated PDF reports are primarily stored locally on the user’s device for viewing, sharing, or personal management. If you choose to send a PDF using the iOS sharing feature, that sharing activity is handled according to the rules of the third-party service you select.

5. Permissions

CarComfort requires access to sensor data related to Motion & Fitness to read device motion information during measurements and calculate vehicle ride vibration and comfort metrics. Without this permission, the app cannot perform measurements.

6. Children’s Privacy

CarComfort is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect children’s personal data.

7. Third-Party Services

CarComfort does not include built-in third-party advertising tracking services. If future versions introduce new cloud or analytics features, this policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Your Control

You can at any time:

  • Choose not to start a measurement
  • Delete locally generated PDFs or related data
  • Disable Motion & Fitness permission in iPhone system settings

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the support page or the contact information provided by the developer.
