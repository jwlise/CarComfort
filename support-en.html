CarComfort Support

Last updated: 2026-04-18

Thank you for using CarComfort. If you encounter problems with measurements, report exports, or the released version of the app, please refer to the guidance below.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why can’t I start a measurement?

Please first check that:

  • Your device supports Device Motion
  • You have allowed CarComfort to access motion sensor data
  • You have tapped “Prepare” or completed the measurement settings

2. Why are no measurement results displayed?

CarComfort displays comfort, motion sickness risk, and other metrics only after a measurement has started and valid samples have been collected. Before a measurement starts, the screen displays a waiting-for-measurement message.

3. Why can’t I export a PDF?

Please check that:

  • You have completed at least one valid measurement
  • Your device has enough available storage
  • The iOS share sheet is not restricted by the system

4. Can measurement results be used for formal certification?

CarComfort is primarily intended for research, prototype validation, road segment comparisons, and observing trends. For formal regulatory validation, compliance testing, or high-precision engineering certification, professional measurement equipment and complete standardized procedures are still recommended.

Supported Features

  • In-vehicle vibration measurement
  • Assessment of comfort and motion sickness risk trends
  • PDF report export
  • Interfaces in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, English, German, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, and Arabic
